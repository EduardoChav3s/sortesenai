Audience/job: friends of one SENAI class on phones; job = roletar a card, check collection, earn coins.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the collection is a sticker album; every card is a numbered die-cut sticker and the reel deals stickers into an empty album slot; refuses the dark neon casino gacha and the glass dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: album page in near-white paper, ink near-black violet, one color per album section (Roletar tomato red, Coleção sunshine yellow, Jogos grass green, Loja tangerine, Ranking grape, Perfil bubblegum), blue only as a minor section color. Stickers: white die-cut border, printed tier color, number badge, name plate with one-line note; epic = silver foil, legendary = gold foil, mythic = holographic. Empty slots are printed frames with the number. Buttons: chunky solid pills with a darker bottom ledge that presses down, no gloss. Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 for display and numbers, Figtree for UI.
STORY: the player sees coins and luck, presses Roletar, the strip of stickers slides into the empty slot and the winner sticks in; the mural shows who got a shiny one.
FIRST VIEWPORT: left 248px ink sidebar (album cover: wordmark, page list each with its section color chip, profile at foot). Main: page title with its red section chip, coin and luck chips right. Center: white album panel with the reel; the window is a printed empty slot frame (dashed corners, number) at 1.25 scale; under it the chunky red Roletar button with coin cost. Bottom: mural strip.
FORM: album de figurinhas, IMPECCABLE PICK (position 1 of 7 on my list), chosen by the owner over the assigned caderno com marca-texto; seed:4cc9695a866a4036
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
